Standard Basic of $\mathbb{R}^n$
There is one for every $n$

Standard Matrix of $T: \mathbb{R}^n \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^m$

Standard matrix of a linear transformation $T: \mathbb{R}^n \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^m$
$T$ expects $[a,b]$
$T([a,b])=T([a,0])+T([0,b])$
$T([a,b])=aT([1,0])+bT([0,1])$
This is just a matrix times a vector

$$
\left[
\begin{array}{cc}
T\left(\left[
\begin{array}{cc}
1 \\ 0
\end{array}
\right]\right) && T\left(\left[
\begin{array}{cc}
0 \\ 1
\end{array}
\right]\right)
\end{array}
\right]
\left[
\begin{array}{cc}
a \\ b
\end{array}

\right]
$$
This is the standard matrix of $T$
