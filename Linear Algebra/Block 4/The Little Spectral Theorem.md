A $n\times n$ matrix is diagonalizable if and only if there is a basis of $\mathbb{R}^n$ consisting of eigen vectors of $A$

A basis of $\mathbb{R}^n$ consisting of eigen vectors of $A$ is an "eigen basis"

When is there an eigen basis

A spectral theorem is a theorem that tells you when there is an eigen basis

$\lambda_1,\lambda_2,...,\lambda_k$ are distinct eigen values of $A$, with corresponding eigenvectors $v_1,v_2,...,v_k$
Then this list of eigenvectors is linearly independent

If $A$ is $n\times n$ and $A$ has $n$ distinct eigen values, then there is an eigen basis of $A$ for $\mathbb{R}^n$ so $A$ is diagonalizable
