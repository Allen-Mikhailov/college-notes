How to determine the eigen values of a matrix

An eigen value is when $(A-bI)x=0$ 
- has a non trivial solution
- is not invertible
- determinant $\neq0$
- $b$ is a root of the characteristic polynomial of $A$
$b\in \mathbb{R}$ 

$$
A=\begin{bmatrix}
-2 & 8 \\
-2 & 4
\end{bmatrix},
det\left(\begin{bmatrix}
-2-\lambda & 8 \\
-2 & 4-\lambda
\end{bmatrix}
\right)=0
$$
This determinant will be some polynomial
The value where the determinant is $0$ are the eigen values of the matrix
