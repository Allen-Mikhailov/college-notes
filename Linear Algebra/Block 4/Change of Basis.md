You can change he basis of a vector from one basis to another using a basis
$dim(V)=n, basis\;\mathbb{B}, basis\;\mathbb{C}$
$v \in V, [v]_{\mathbb{B}}\in \mathbb{R}^n, [v]_{\mathbb{B}}\in \mathbb{R}^n$

Transformation $P$ is $\mathbb{C} \leftarrow \mathbb{B}$
$P([v]_{\mathbb{B}})=[v]_{\mathbb{C}}$
it is always a matrix

How to actually make the matrix

$P$ is also a matrix here
$i^{th}$ col. of $P=P e_i=P [b_i]_{\mathbb{B}}=[b_i]_{\mathbb{C}}$

$$
P=\left[
\begin{array}{cc}
| & | \\
[b_1]_{\mathbb{C}} & [b_2]_{\mathbb{C}} \\
| & | \\
\end{array}
\right]
$$
