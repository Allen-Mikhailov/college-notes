A transpose of a matrix is when you flip the rows and columns. The rows become the columns and the columns become the rows

$A_{mxn}$,  $B_{nxp}$  , $AB_{mxp}$
$B^T_{pxn}$,  $A^T_{nxm}$, $B^TA^T_{pxm}$

$B^TA^T=(AB)^T$

### Transposes and Inverses
$A=B^{-1}$
$AB=I=BA$
$(AB)^T=I^T=(BA)^T$
$B^TA^T=I=A^TB^T$

If $A$ is invertible then $A^T$ is invertible and it goes back so if $A^T$ is invertible then $(A^T)^T$ is invertible and $(A^T)^T=A$

If the rows of $A$ are lineally independent then the columns of $A$ are linearly independent
