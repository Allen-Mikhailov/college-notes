They all look like this
$ax^2y''+bxy'+cy=0$

How to solve:

Guess $y=x^m$
$y'=mx^{m-1}$
$y''=m(m-1)x^{m-2}$

While the powers get reduced here, but when you plug them back in they will get increased back to m

Plug it back in
$ax^2m(m-1)x^{m-2}+bxmx^{m-1}+cx^m$
$am(m-1)x^m+bmx^m+cx^m$
$(am^2-am)x^m+bmx^m+cx^m$
$am^2x^m+(b-a)mx^m+cx^m$
