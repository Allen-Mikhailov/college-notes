Vector field doesn't depend on time

$$
\begin{bmatrix}
x' \\ y'
\end{bmatrix}
=
\begin{bmatrix}
f(x,y) \\ g(x,y)
\end{bmatrix}
$$

Vector field
$\vec{v}(x,y)=\begin{bmatrix}f(x,y) \\ g(x,y)\end{bmatrix}$
Integral curves of $\vec{v}(x,y)$ are the solutions curves of the D.E.

If this can be separated and integrated we get solution curves directly
$\frac{dy/dt}{dx/dt}=\frac{g(x,y)}{f(x,y)}$

Example:
$x'=y^2,y'=-x$
$\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{-x}{y^2}$
Solution curves
$3x^2+2y^3=C$
