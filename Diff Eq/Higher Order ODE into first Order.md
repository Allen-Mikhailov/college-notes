
Example $x''+x=0$
$y=x'$ (Lets try this)
$y'=x''$
$x''=-x$

Now we have a system
$y=x'$
$y'=-x$
Matrix form
$$
\left[
\begin{array}{c}
x' \\ y'
\end{array}
\right]
=\left[
\begin{array}{cc}
0 & 1 \\ -1 & 0
\end{array}
\right]
\left[
\begin{array}{c}
x \\ y
\end{array}
\right]
$$
This is solvable easily
