A datasheet is the contract between you and the manufacturer on the characteristics of the components
- summary of features
- Electrical Characteristics
- Functional / Application Details
- Mechanical Information
Additional details for more complex components can be found in a "System Reference Manual" (SRM) or "Technical Reference Manual" (TRM)
- Example uses can be found in example notes
**Datasheet is your friend**
Save a copy of the one you designed with, because mistakes can be made and things will be updated
Look for "interesting points"
- min/max current that changes the behavior of the component
- voltage where the behavior changes
- Inflection points
- Understand what will happen over time
